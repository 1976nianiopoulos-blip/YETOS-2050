## Philosophical origin

The pre-existing Alchemist Negentropy Matrix is a broader
syntropic agroforestry and ecological-regeneration vision.

YETOS 2050 is a separate NASA Space Apps prototype,
inspired by environmental and intergenerational ethics.

Inspirations include Aristotle, Masanobu Fukuoka,
Erwin Schrodinger's discussion of negative entropy,
and the Oromo Safuu/Gadaa ethical traditions.

These influences are design motivations, not physical
model inputs or proof of scientific predictions.
No private TelosX code is incorporated.
