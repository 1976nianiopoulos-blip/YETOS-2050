YETOS 2050 asks a simple question:
What happens when the same rain meets different soils?

Using NASA POWER historical weather, we built
a reproducible daily water-balance comparison.

Two hypothetical soils produce different
water-storage and drainage responses.

We also test a two-month no-rainfall shock.

Our Evidence Passport separates verified calculations
from assumptions and missing field data.

YETOS is a transparent exploratory prototype,
not a validated farm recommendation system.
